# The Deeper Ocean

Welcome to the Icathia, where Kassadin walks in the void. The inscription you are seeing resonates with the purpose of the world, for a more creative style not interfered by AI. Most of the things here, can be inferred what should be meant, but not be deduced what can be concluded.

It has been planned, since given instructions have been made in the past, that what remains to be presented is chaos. Unnecessary Floating Objects embezzling the space, left to be lit from darkness in no time.

Take a comfy seat. Find a theme online, so that your screen has been rendered black and green. Stones from monoliths are falling down, thunders are piercing through the clouds, covering the eminent light from a new dawn. It's a volcano.

Prophecy from past interferes with present. Lava pours down from the sky, freezing into piles of glowing wax and then become earth and stone. Architects pick a handful of lava to burn, a handful of wax to sculpt, and a handful of stone to construct. We cannot decide what to give up and what to add in.
